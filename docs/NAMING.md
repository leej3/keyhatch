# Name exploration

Working recommendation: **Handrail**.

The product should feel useful and approachable, not militarized or magical.
It helps people move confidently while defining a boundary.
It is an interconnect and permission layer, not another password manager.

| Name | What it communicates | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Handrail | Helpful, practical support and a boundary | Needs a credential-oriented subtitle |
| Keybridge | Connects existing passwords to tools | Literal but generic; may imply simple forwarding |
| Keyhatch | A small, deliberate opening for credentials | Friendly but less immediately clear |
| Latch | Simple, controlled access | Very common word; collision risk |
| Passalong | Helpful sharing without a new vault service | Could imply uncontrolled disclosure |
| Sill | A clear threshold | Short but too abstract for discovery |

Candidate copy:

- Handrail — Your passwords.
  A smaller reach for your agents.
- Handrail — Practical permissions for local agents.
- Handrail — Keep Passwords.
  Choose what your agents can use.

Avoid “rogue-agent proof,” “passwords never leave the enclave,” or “revokes
leaked credentials.”
These are not defensible claims.

This is creative exploration,
**not trademark, domain, package-name, or App Store availability clearance**.
Check those before choosing a public brand.
The repository name is provisional and can change without touching user state.
